# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

AI builders and AI researchers are equally important audiences. They use the product to investigate model performance and compare options across benchmarks, metrics, and data sources on desktop or mobile.

## Product Purpose

The product brings different LLM benchmarks and data sources into one place, then turns them into useful, interactive graphs. Users can compare models by quality, cost, speed, and additional metrics, explore tradeoffs, and understand how results differ across sources. A successful visit gives someone the graphs and evidence they need to make a model decision or continue deeper research.

## Positioning

One clear workspace for comparing LLMs across multiple benchmarks and data sources instead of consulting isolated leaderboards.

## Brand Personality

Simple, minimal, and scientific. The product should feel credible and focused while making exploration approachable.

## Anti-references

Avoid complicated interfaces that expose too many controls at once, obscure the graph, or make comparison feel like operating a dense enterprise dashboard.

## Design Principles

- Put comparison first: every screen should help users understand meaningful differences between models.
- Make sources visible: clearly identify where each metric and benchmark result comes from.
- Reveal complexity progressively: keep the default view simple while supporting deeper research when requested.
- Let the data lead: graphs and evidence carry the interface; decoration stays restrained.
- Work at every viewport: preserve useful comparison and exploration on mobile, not just desktop.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use strong accessibility defaults throughout: complete keyboard navigation, readable contrast, color-blind-safe graph encodings, non-color indicators, reduced-motion support, semantic structure, and clear focus states.
